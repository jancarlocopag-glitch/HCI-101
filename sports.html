About this Genre

Sports manga centers on athletic competition, training, and teamwork, focusing on the journey of athletes as they work hard to improve their skills and achieve their goals. These stories often highlight the dedication, discipline, and perseverance required in sports, showing characters overcoming physical and mental challenges through practice, determination, and support from teammates. Along the way, athletes face intense rivalries, personal setbacks, and moments of self-doubt, which help shape their growth both on and off the field. Through these experiences, sports manga emphasizes themes of personal improvement, sportsmanship, resilience, and the strong bonds formed between teammates who share the same passion for victory.
