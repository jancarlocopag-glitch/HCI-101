About this Genre

Adventure manga follows characters on exciting and often dangerous journeys where they explore new places, discover unfamiliar worlds, and face various challenges along the way. These stories usually focus on a main goal, such as searching for treasure, protecting others, or fulfilling a dream, which drives the characters to keep moving forward. Throughout their travels, they encounter powerful enemies, unexpected obstacles, and new allies who become important friends. As the journey progresses, the characters grow stronger, gain new skills, and develop deeper bonds with their companions. Because of this, adventure manga not only highlights thrilling exploration and action but also emphasizes friendship, perseverance, and the personal growth that comes from overcoming difficult challenges.
